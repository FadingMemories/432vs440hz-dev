About Hemi Sync

What this tool shows

Hemi Sync uses slightly different tones in the left and right channels to create a perceived beat frequency. This page visualizes that relationship as wave motion, stereo phase geometry and generative patterns.

How to use it

Select a band preset or enter two frequencies manually. Use headphones at a comfortable volume if you want to hear stereo separation. The visualizations are educational and do not claim guaranteed cognitive, medical or therapeutic effects.
